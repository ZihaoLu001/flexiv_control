# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: being
respectful and considerate, giving and gracefully accepting constructive
feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political
attacks.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers via the repository's **Security → Report a
vulnerability** channel or a direct message to a maintainer. All complaints will
be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

The full text and enforcement guidelines are available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
